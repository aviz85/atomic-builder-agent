# Layer 0 Discovery

Use this skill to clarify the student's human nucleus.

Layer 0 is not motivation fluff. It defines what should drive the whole agentic system.

## Ask In Small Batches

Ask up to 3 questions at a time.

## Core Questions

1. What do you do that should never be fully delegated?
2. What decisions require your taste, judgment, relationships, or intuition?
3. What drains you but does not require your unique value?
4. What part of the business feels trapped inside your head?
5. What would you love the agent to handle every morning before you start work?
6. What would make you trust an agent more?
7. What would make you stop trusting it?
8. What business outcome do you want to unlock in the next 30 days?
9. What is one project that would prove this method works for you?
10. If your business had a control room, what would be on the main screen?

## Output

Write or update `data/student-profile.md` with:

- Unique value.
- Delegable work.
- Non-delegable work.
- Trust boundaries.
- First project candidate.
- Energy statement.
- Layer 0 risks.
